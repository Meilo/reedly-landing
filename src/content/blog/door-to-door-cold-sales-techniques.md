You knock on the door. The prospect opens. You have seven seconds before they decide whether you're worth listening to or just another interruption in their day.

Door-to-door prospecting in B2B isn't dead. It's just that most people do it wrong. They show up unannounced, launch into a pitch about themselves, and wonder why the door closes in their face.

Here's what actually works when you're doing field prospecting B2B in 2024.

## 1. Show up with a reason, not a sales pitch

"I was in the area" doesn't cut it. "I noticed your trucks outside and wanted to ask about your fleet maintenance" does. The first thirty seconds aren't about you or your product. They're about proving you've done your homework and you're not wasting their time.

## 2. Use the lobby test

Before you knock, spend two minutes observing. Is there construction going on? New signage? A delivery truck unloading? These are conversation starters that show you pay attention. One rep I know landed a €40K deal because he noticed a competitor's equipment being unloaded and asked, "Expanding capacity?"

## 3. Ask for fifteen minutes, not a sale

Nobody has time for a full presentation. Everyone has fifteen minutes. Be specific: "I need fifteen minutes to understand if what we do makes sense for you. If it doesn't, I'll tell you." Then stick to it. Walking away after fourteen minutes builds more trust than any cold calling technique.

## 4. Map the building while you're there

Even if the first contact says no, ask who else in the organization handles [specific function]. Get names. Ask where their office is. You're not being pushy, you're being thorough. Half of door-to-door prospecting is intelligence gathering.

## 5. Leave something they'll keep

Not a brochure. Something useful. A one-page benchmark, a checklist, a local regulation summary. Make it specific to their industry and small enough to pin on a wall. You want to still be there when you're not.

## 6. Book the follow-up before you leave

"Can I call you next Tuesday to see if you've had time to think about it?" is weak. "I'll send you the case study we discussed and call you Thursday at 10. Does that work?" shows you're serious. Get the commitment while you're face-to-face.

## 7. Track everything immediately

The second you're back in the car, record what happened. Who you spoke to, what they said, what you promised. Waiting until evening means you'll forget the detail that matters. The objection they almost didn't mention. The competitor they're unhappy with.

## 8. Go back

Most field reps visit once and give up. The real wins come on visit three or four, when you're not a stranger anymore. You're the person who keeps showing up with useful information. Persistence beats perfection in door-to-door prospecting.

The best B2B field sales reps don't treat every door the same. They observe, adapt, and follow through. The door is just the beginning.
